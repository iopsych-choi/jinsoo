Research

Publications

My research spans three main areas: (1) Personnel Selection — the rigorous development and validation of pre-employment tools and fake-resistant measures, and applying multi-objective optimization to address the validity-fairness dilemma; (2) Research Methods — the application of AI in general psychology and personnel selection, alongside SEM models/techniques for examining hierarchical psychological constructs; and (3) Well-Being — enhancing subjective well-being by improving job satisfaction and making work feel more meaningful.
