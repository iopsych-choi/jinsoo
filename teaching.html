Teaching

As an I/O psychologist, I am committed to the "scientist-practitioner" model, integrating scientific findings with practical applications in the classroom. My teaching philosophy is to create a safe learning environment where students can read, think, and share their ideas freely, in a manner that is both critical and respectful of one another.
